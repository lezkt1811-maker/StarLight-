Privacy at Lilith and Eve Astrology

Chart generation

Your birth date, time, and location are used in your browser to calculate your chart. This information is not sent anywhere unless you choose to request a personalized reading.

Personalized readings

If you purchase the $25 detailed reading or the $7 Lilith & Eve placement reading, your complete generated chart — including your name, email address, birth date, exact birth time, birth location, planetary positions, and a snapshot image of your chart wheel — is sent privately to our systems for the purpose of preparing and emailing your personalized PDF. This information is delivered privately and is not published anywhere.

Payment is processed by Stripe. We do not collect or store your card number or other complete payment credentials — Stripe handles that directly, and we only receive confirmation that payment succeeded.

Referral codes

If you arrive through a promoter's link, a referral code is stored in your browser for up to 30 days so that promoter can be credited if you purchase a reading. A referral code never changes your price.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't store your birth information permanently in your browser.
  • We don't put your personal information in public files, URLs, or analytics.

Questions

Contact us using the email address shown on the order confirmation page.
