FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to supply anything?

For the $50 service-only option, yes: the correct oil and filter. For full-service appointments, I bring them.

How long does an oil change take?

Most appointments should take approximately 30–60 minutes depending on the vehicle and access.

Can you change oil on every vehicle?

No. Most common passenger vehicles can be serviced, but some vehicles require specialty equipment or procedures. Vehicle details are verified before the appointment.

Where do you perform the oil change?

At homes, workplaces and other approved safe parking locations.

What happens to the old oil?

Used motor oil is collected and taken for proper recycling.

Can I stay inside?

Yes. Once access to the vehicle is arranged, you don't need to sit outside watching the entire service.
